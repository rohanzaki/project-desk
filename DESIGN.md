# Project Desk
A working desk for the human owner, Codex, and Claude. Task rows are the central object;
messages and decisions sit beside them. No marketing hero or decorative charts.
Palette: paper #f7f9fc, ink #172b46, navy #234675, blue #2459c4,
sage #28704e, amber #8c570c. Segoe UI for working text, Georgia for the desk title.
Layout: left aligned task ledger (two thirds), conversation/notebook (one third).
On mobile, a single stack. Status, ownership, and next step are visible together.
Review: avoid a card grid; use a continuous task list with contextual actions.
